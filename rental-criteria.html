Rental Criteria

Criteria may vary by property. Leasing will confirm the standard that applies to the home you want.

Generally required

  • Completed application for each adult occupant
  • Government-issued photo identification
  • Income sufficient to support the rent (often about 2.5–3× monthly rent combined)
  • Verifiable rental or housing history
  • Authorization for screening permitted by law

Fair housing

SilverOak Residential Properties provides Equal Housing Opportunity. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, familial status, or any other characteristic protected by federal, state, or local law.
